Google ML Kit (GenAI Prompt API, com.google.mlkit:genai-prompt 1.0.0-beta4 and its ML Kit and
Google Play services dependencies)

GMind uses ML Kit only to ask Android's own Gemini Nano model (AICore) to answer on the phone, on
phones where Android offers it. ML Kit is provided by Google under the ML Kit Terms of Service:
https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms
It is not open-source software; its terms, and Google's APIs Terms of Service
(https://developers.google.com/terms), apply to that component only.

ML Kit's usage logging (Google's datatransport "cct" upload backend) is removed from GMind's
manifest, so GMind does not send ML Kit usage data. The Gemini Nano model itself is managed and
updated by Android, not by GMind.
